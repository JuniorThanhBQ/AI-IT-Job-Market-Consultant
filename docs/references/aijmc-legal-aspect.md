# AIJMC LEGAL AGREEMENT AND TERMS OF SERVICE

**Last updated:** 22 August 2026 <br>
**Host Organization:** Van Trung Thanh

## 1. INTRODUCTION AND OPERATING PRINCIPLES
AI IT Jobs Market Consultant (AIJMC) is a scientific research and graduation thesis project at Ho Chi Minh City Open University. AIJMC's operating principle is to follow the Intellectual Property Law of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, and the Term of Services of current job platforms.

### 1.1. Non-commercial purpose
The AIJMC system is operated for academic and scientific research. The system does not provide direct job application functions, does not charge service fees, does not sell data and is not for any commercial or profit-making purposes.

### 1.2. Short-term nature
The system is deployed in a limited access environment or public for a short amount of time. This is only for testing and thesis grading.

## 2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA COLLECTION POLICY
The system collects data about jobs and companies from recruitment platforms including ITviec, VietnamWorks, TopDev, and ITJobs. AIJMC.

### 2.1. Copyright Exception
The collection and display of information by AIJMC are based on **Point a, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law of Vietnam (amended and supplemented in 2022)** about using published works without asking for permission or paying royalties.

### 2.2. Following robots technical standards
The system only collects public data from websites, does not require user accounts, does not break security, and follows the robots.txt rules of the source platforms to not affect their servers.

### 2.3. Respecting content ownership
All job descriptions, images, logos, brand names, and related data are the intellectual property of ITJobs, ITviec, TopDev, VietnamWorks platforms and the original employers. AIJMC does not claim ownership of any displayed data.

## 3. REDIRECTION AND PROTECTING ORIGINAL PLATFORMS' RIGHTS

### 3.1. Traffic Redirection
The system does not have an “Apply” button. Every job posting on AIJMC includes a direct link to the original post on platforms like ITviec, VietnamWorks, TopDev, and ITJobs. This protects the rights and traffic of the content-owning websites.

### 3.2. No competitive behavior
AIJMC understands the anti-competition rules from the platforms. AIJMC promises that it is not a legal entity or a job service business, and does not compete directly or indirectly with anyone.

## 4. USER DATA PRIVACY
AIJMC does not ask users to register accounts with sensitive personal data like a citizen identification card, does not store long-term candidate profiles, does not store employer information for fees, and therefore does not process or illegally share any personal data under the personal data protection decree.

## 5. DISCLAIMER OF LIABILITY

*   **Information accuracy:** Because the data is automatically taken from third-party websites, AIJMC does not guarantee the completeness, update status, or accuracy of the job postings.
*   **Exemption from liability:** AIJMC will not be legally responsible for any direct or indirect loss or damage caused by users accessing the system or relying on the job information on the platform.
*   **External links:** AIJMC does not control the content and is not responsible for the links pointing outside its website.

## 6. COMPLAINT RESOLUTION AND TAKEDOWN POLICY
Although AIJMC works based on fair use in scientific research, AIJMC respects the Terms of Service and commercial rights of the data-owning companies.

*   **Takedown process:** If ITviec, VietnamWorks, TopDev, or ITJobs platforms find that information on AIJMC affects their business, please send an email to the support contact.
