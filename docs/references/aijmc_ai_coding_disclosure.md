## Statement on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in the Graduation Thesis

Author's Commitment: Van Trung Thanh <br>
Related Content: AIJMC and Graduation Thesis

I confirm the use of artificial intelligence in my graduation thesis product and report. Specifically, the methods used are as follows:

### For the Product:

1. I used Antigravity for programming with Gemini Flash versions 3.5, 3.6, and 3.7 as the main model. The method I used consisted of 5 processes:

- Overview of the problem to be solved
- Requesting the AI ​​to create a detailed plan
- Accepting or requesting modifications to the plan
- The AI ​​executes the plan
- Reviewing the generated code.

2. Simultaneously, the web versions of chatGPT, Gemini, and Claude were also used as references in designing functionality or debugging difficult errors.

### For the thesis report:

1. NotebookLM was used to synthesize and search for the desired research content.
2. Grammarly was used to check Vietnamese/English spelling.
3. In addition, for some sentences where I lacked ideas for paraphrasing, I used chatGPT for reference.

### Author's Commitment:
1. I commit that all AI-generated content has been checked, edited, and supplemented by me.
2. Regarding the product, I confirm that I used source code generated by artificial intelligence. To address this shortcoming, I will control the implement of system design, source code review, version control, co-validation, E2E tests and CI/CD processes to ensure quality control.
3. For the thesis report, I guarantee that the use of AI is limited to references with over 80% of the content written by me.
