# AIJMC LEGAL AGREEMENT AND TERMS OF SERVICE

**Last updated:** August 2026
**Host Organization:** Van Trung Thanh from Ho Chi Minh City Open University

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION AND OPERATING PRINCIPLES
AIJMC (AI IT Jobs Market Consultant) is a scientific research and graduation thesis project at Ho Chi Minh City Open University. AIJMC's operating principle is to strictly follow the laws of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, especially the Intellectual Property Law, and the rules of current job platforms.

### 1.1. Non-commercial purpose
The AIJMC system is operated **completely for academic, scientific research, and personal use**. The system does not provide direct job application functions, does not charge service fees, does not sell data, and is not for any commercial or profit-making purposes.

### 1.2. Short-term nature
The system is deployed in a limited access environment or public for a short amount of time. This is only for testing and thesis grading. It will be closed or restricted after the project is completed.

---

## 2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA COLLECTION POLICY
The system collects data about jobs and companies from recruitment platforms including but not limited to ITviec, VietnamWorks, TopDev, and ITJobs. AIJMC commits:

### 2.1. Copyright Exception (Fair Use)
The collection and display of information by AIJMC are based on **Point a, Clause 1, Article 25 of the Intellectual Property Law of Vietnam (amended and supplemented in 2022)** about using published works without asking for permission or paying royalties: *"Making one copy for personal scientific research or teaching purposes"*.
For the ITviec platform, the system operates on the principle of *"reasonable personal use"*.

### 2.2. Following Internet technical standards (Robots.txt)
The system only collects public data from websites, does not require user accounts, does not break security, and **strictly follows the `robots.txt` rules** of the source platforms to not affect their servers.

### 2.3. Respecting content ownership
All job descriptions, images, logos, brand names, and related data are the intellectual property of the recruitment platforms (ITJobs, ITviec, TopDev, VietnamWorks) and the original employers. AIJMC does not claim ownership of any displayed data.

---

## 3. REDIRECTION AND PROTECTING ORIGINAL PLATFORMS' RIGHTS

### 3.1. Traffic Redirection
The system does not have an "Apply" button. Every job posting on AIJMC **must include a direct link (URL) to the original post** on platforms like ITviec, VietnamWorks, TopDev, ITJobs. This protects the rights and traffic of the content-owning websites.

### 3.2. No competitive behavior
AIJMC clearly understands the anti-competition rules from the platforms (for example: forbidding HR companies or e-commerce platforms from exploiting information). AIJMC promises it is not a legal entity or a job service business, and does not compete directly or indirectly with anyone.

---

## 4. USER DATA PRIVACY
AIJMC does not ask users to register accounts with sensitive personal data like citizen identification card, does not storage long term candidate profiles (CVs), does not store employer information for fees, and therefore, does not process or illegally share any personal data under the personal data protection decree (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP).

*   **External links:** AIJMC does not control the content and is not responsible for the links pointing outside its website.

---

## 6. COMPLAINT RESOLUTION AND TAKEDOWN POLICY
Although AIJMC works based on fair use in scientific research, we completely respect the Terms of Service and commercial rights of the data-owning companies.

*   **Takedown process:** If platforms (ITviec, VietnamWorks, TopDev, ITJobs, etc.) find that information on AIJMC affects their business, please send a written request (email) to the research team.
*   **Commitment to act:** The AIJMC management team promises to immediately check, respond kindly, and delete data, block domains, or close related data collection streams as soon as possible to solve legal issues.
